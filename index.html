What they do(info)

Bees help pollinate plants. As they drink nectar, pollen will stick to their body. Pollen falls of as the bee travels from flower to flower

Bees make honey. They store the nectar they drank in their body to bring back to their hive. they would then pass the nectar around and fan it with their wings to dry excess water, making honey in the process (they store the honey in the honeycomb after completion).

Their impact(info)

They supply our food!

Since bees help with pollinating plants, they supply a bunch of our food.

  • fruits: Apple, strawberries, watermelons
  • vegetables: broccoli, cucumber, pumpkins
  • Bonus: they also pollinate plants (e.g. clover) that farm animals (e.g. cow) eat

They maintain the ecosystem!

Other than just helping plants pollinate to produce food, they also help with the production of seeds. Plants grow and can act as shelter for animals and perform photosynthesis to produce oxygen, so we can breathe.

They are essential to our economy!

  • Products: Bees create valuable products such as honey, beeswax that are used as food and remedies
  • Employment: Bees enable jobs like beekepers
  • Algriculture: Bees help a ton with pollination which allows businesses to sell and make money from it.
